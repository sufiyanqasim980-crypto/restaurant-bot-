This Privacy Policy explains how AK foods Cafe ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you contact our restaurant through its WhatsApp ordering assistant or visit this website.

1. Information We May Collect

When you chat with our WhatsApp assistant, we may receive and process information you choose to send, including:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the messages you send.
  • Your name and order details, such as menu items, quantities, and order preferences.
  • Delivery or pickup details, including your address, area, landmark, and contact instructions.
  • Booking details, such as requested date, time, and number of guests, if you make a reservation request.
  • Payment confirmation information, such as a transaction ID (TID), if you choose an electronic payment method.

Please do not send passwords, card PINs, one-time passcodes, or other highly sensitive financial credentials in chat.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • Answer questions about our menu, prices, opening hours, and services.
  • Prepare, confirm, and manage food orders and reservation requests.
  • Arrange delivery or pickup and communicate order updates.
  • Verify payment references and send relevant order details to authorized restaurant staff.
  • Maintain chat context so the assistant can respond appropriately during a conversation.
  • Protect the service, troubleshoot errors, and prevent misuse.

3. WhatsApp and Service Providers

Our assistant uses the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta to send and receive WhatsApp messages. Information sent through WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp's and Meta's applicable terms and privacy policies.

We use Google Gemini to help generate automated responses to customer messages. Relevant message content may be sent to Google's AI service for this purpose and is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Our website and server functions are hosted by Netlify. Chat history and related service data may be stored using Netlify's storage services. These providers process information to provide their services to us.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell customer personal information. We may share relevant information:

  • With authorized restaurant staff so they can prepare or manage your order or booking.
  • With the service providers described above, where needed to operate the assistant.
  • When required by applicable law or a valid legal process, or when necessary to protect rights, safety, and the service.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Chat messages and order context may be stored by our hosting and storage provider so the assistant can maintain conversation history and process requests. We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary for restaurant operations, customer support, recordkeeping, and security, unless a longer period is required by law.

Retention periods may depend on the type of information and operational requirements. You may contact us to ask about deletion of information, subject to legal and legitimate business recordkeeping requirements.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No online service or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You can choose what information to provide. If you do not provide details needed for an order or booking, we may be unable to complete that request. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

Our ordering assistant is intended for people making restaurant inquiries and orders. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
